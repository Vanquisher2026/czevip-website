Our story

Hand-embroidered, never stamped.

CZEVIP is a Brooklyn-based hat label built around a simple idea: family caps should be made the same way as fine hats - one stitch at a time, finished by hand, not pulled off a production line.

Each piece is hand-embroidered with the word that matters to you - dad, mom, wife - in white on garment-washed cotton twill. Designed in New York, manufactured through a small network of audited workshops, and shipped from our Brooklyn office with a lifetime craftsmanship guarantee.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you ship internationally?

Yes - we ship to over 60 countries via DHL and FedEx. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for most destinations.

What is your return policy?

Unworn hats in original condition can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Custom and monogrammed pieces are final sale.

Can I order a custom word or color?

Yes - we offer custom embroidery and Pantone color matching for orders of 50 units or more. Email info@czevip.com for a quote.

Do you offer wholesale or OEM?

Yes. We manufacture for brands and retailers worldwide. See our Manufacturing page or apply to the Partner program.
