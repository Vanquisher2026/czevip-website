Which hat is right?

Hat comparisons, side by side.

Honest, no-nonsense comparisons of the most common headwear choices. Pick the right hat for your face, the weather, and the occasion.

Rule of thumb: if you wear it once a week, get a fedora; if you wear it twice a month in summer, get a panama; if you want one casual hat for weekends, get a trilby.
