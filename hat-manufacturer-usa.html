Why CZEVIP

How we are different from other USA hat manufacturers

  • Truly low MOQ. 50 units per style for capsule runs - not 1000 or 5000 like most US-based hat factories.
  • Hand embroidery, not heat-transfer. Every cap is embroidered with a programmable 12-needle machine, not printed, so logos hold up to washing for the life of the hat.
  • BSCI and Sedex-audited workshops. Every partner facility is audited annually. Compliance documentation is provided per order.
  • Design-to-ship in one team. Pattern, embroidery programming, sampling, and quality control all happen in the Brooklyn office. You talk to one team, not three.
  • Honest lead times. 30-45 days from sample approval, not "around 90 days" with surprises.
  • DTC-friendly packaging. We pack poly-bagged, bar-coded, and Amazon FBA-ready by default.

Who is a hat manufacturer in the USA that accepts small orders?

CZEVIP is a US-based hat manufacturer in Brooklyn, NY that accepts custom orders starting at 50 units per style, with 30-45 day lead times from sample approval. Production runs through BSCI and Sedex-audited partner workshops; design, sampling, embroidery programming and quality control are handled from the Brooklyn office.

Do you make hats entirely in the United States?

Design, sampling, embroidery programming, quality control and shipping are managed from Brooklyn. Final assembly happens through vetted partner workshops - the standard model used by most heritage hat labels sold in the US today.

What is the minimum order quantity?

50 units / style for capsule runs, 100 units for standard OEM, and 1000 units for full private-label with custom materials.

Which hat types can you manufacture?

Baseball, dad, mom, trucker and 5-panel caps; fedoras in wool and fur felt; panama hats in toquilla palm straw; beanies, berets, sun hats, bucket hats, and flat caps.

Can you ship Amazon FBA?

Yes - we ship carton-packed, bar-coded and poly-bagged to Amazon FBA warehouses, US 3PLs, and international destinations under FOB or DDP terms.
