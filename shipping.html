Support

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Orders ship within 1-3 business days from our warehouse.
  • Free worldwide shipping on orders over $150.
  • Standard delivery: 5-9 business days. Express: 2-4 business days.
  • Duties and taxes calculated at checkout for most destinations.
  • Tracking link emailed once the carrier scans the parcel.

Returns

  • 30-day returns on unworn hats in original condition.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer; original shipping is non-refundable.
  • Refunds processed within 5 business days of receipt.
  • Custom, monogrammed, and bespoke pieces are final sale.

Exchanges

Free size exchange within 30 days for stock styles. Contact us first and we will email a prepaid label.

Lifetime reshape

Every CZEVIP hat comes with a lifetime reshape guarantee. Send it back any time for free re-shaping, re-blocking, or sweatband replacement.

Damaged or wrong item

If your hat arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, email info@czevip.com within 7 days. We will replace or refund at no cost.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout for orders shipped to US states where CZEVIP LLC is required to collect tax. The displayed rate reflects current state and local requirements. International orders may be subject to import duties and VAT, calculated at checkout where supported.
