# CZEVIP Launch Playbook - Ready-to-Execute Files

This folder contains copy-paste-ready assets for off-site authority building.
Pick a file, open it, follow the steps in order. No editing required.

## Files

| File | Time to execute | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `crunchbase-profile.md` | 10 min | One company record syndicated to PitchBook, Wikipedia, AI engines |
| `trustpilot-setup.md` | 30 min | Star ratings in Google search + TrustBox widget on every product page |
| `reddit-30-day-calendar.md` | 90 days passive | High-authority mentions from DA-91 domain |
| `youtube-scripts.md` | 1 hour to record | 10 short-form scripts compounding into YouTube ranking |

## Why this order

1. Crunchbase - fastest, free, single source-of-truth that 50 other directories auto-pull from
2. Trustpilot - fast, free, gives Google the star signal it needs to rank above non-reviewed competitors
3. Reddit - 90-day slow burn, but single highest compounding off-site asset
4. YouTube - 6-month slow burn, but YouTube citations feed into Gemini and ChatGPT directly

Total budget: $0. Total time to first signal: 7 days.
