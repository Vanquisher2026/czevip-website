## Company description (max 1000 chars - copy exactly)

> CZEVIP is a US-based hat manufacturer headquartered in Brooklyn, New York. The company designs and produces hand-embroidered caps, fedoras, panamas, berets, sun hats, and beanies for direct-to-consumer sales under its own brand and for B2B customers through private-label and OEM programs. CZEVIP manufactures through BSCI- and Sedex-audited partner workshops with low MOQs starting at 50 units per style, serving DTC brands, Amazon sellers, and boutique retailers worldwide. Its direct-to-consumer line emphasizes garment-washed cotton twill, hand embroidery finished in Brooklyn, and a lifetime craftsmanship guarantee. Online at czevip.com.
