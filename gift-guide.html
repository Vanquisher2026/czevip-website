Eight common questions, with direct answers.

What is a good gift for dad who has everything?

A hand-embroidered cotton-twill cap. It is useful every day, it wears in rather than out, and a custom word or short phrase — his nickname, the kids' names, a private joke — makes it unmistakably his. The CZEVIP Dad Cap is garment-washed for a soft, broken-in feel from day one, with an adjustable metal closure so the fit is right for almost any adult male head (21.7 to 23.6 inches / 55 to 60 cm). At $34 with free US shipping over $80, it is also a gift that does not break the bank.

If he already has a Dad Cap, the Fedora in Charcoal is a step up — a heavier wool-felt body, a grosgrain band, and the same low-key Brooklyn aesthetic. Either way, embroidery is what makes a cap feel personal rather than promotional.

Are matching couple hats a good gift?

Yes. Matching couple hats photograph well, they spark a low-stakes conversation, and they are gender-neutral enough to share on anniversaries, holidays, or engagement gifts. The CZEVIP Mom & Dad Set ships two charcoal washed-twill caps in one box and costs $62, which is $6 less than buying them separately.

If the couple is harder to shop for, a single Mom Cap and a single Dad Cap in the same color also work. The point is not that the embroidery is identical — it usually is not — the point is that the silhouette, fabric and color come from the same family.

What is the difference between hand-embroidered and printed caps?

Hand embroidery stitches thread through the fabric, so the letters have soft edges, slight irregularities in stitch length, and tiny knots visible on the inside. The cap face stays soft. Printed or heat-transferred caps have a flat, slightly plastic surface and will peel or crack within months of regular wear.

CZEVIP family caps are hand-embroidered in our partner workshop using Madeira 40-weight thread on garment-washed cotton twill. You can see the difference the moment you flip the cap inside out: small knots, slightly uneven stitch length, and no stiff mesh backing. See our guide on spotting hand embroidery for the full checklist.

How long does a custom embroidered cap take to ship?

In-stock caps without a custom word ship within 2 business days from our Brooklyn studio and arrive in 3-5 days anywhere in the continental US. A cap with a custom embroidered word adds about 5 business days for production. International orders typically take 7-14 days including customs clearance.

If you are buying for a specific date (birthday, anniversary), place the order at least 10 business days ahead for an in-stock cap, or 15 business days ahead if you want a custom word. We can also ship gift-wrapped directly to the recipient — see below.

Can I order a custom word on the cap?

Yes. Email info@czevip.com with the cap style, the word or short phrase (up to about 12 characters works cleanly across the front), and the quantity. Single-cap custom orders start at $18 extra per cap and take about 5 extra business days. Bulk OEM orders with a custom logo are quoted separately through our manufacturing page.

Custom words are best when they are short, lowercase, and not a brand name. “Dad”, “Mom”, “Coach”, a kid's nickname, a city — all work. Longer phrases get crowded at the standard cap-front size.
